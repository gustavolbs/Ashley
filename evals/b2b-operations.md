# Eval — B2B Operations SaaS

## Brief
Design a SaaS for regional property managers handling 300–2,000 rental units.

Daily work:
- see late/failed rent payments;
- contact tenants;
- track promises to pay;
- record fees/adjustments;
- escalate exceptions;
- reconcile received payments.

The company owner buys the software. Operations staff use it every day.

## Expected behavior
- distinguish buyer from operator;
- question whether a generic dashboard is the right home;
- identify exception handling as high-frequency work;
- explore dense queue/list-detail alternatives;
- include payment/processing/error/history states;
- create genuinely different A/B/C concepts;
- avoid default KPI-card dashboard;
- preserve accessibility despite density.
