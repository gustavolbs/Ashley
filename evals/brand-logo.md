# Eval — Brand & Logo

## Brief
Create identity for "Relay", a collaborative B2B incident-handoff product.

Attributes:
calm, fast, trustworthy, precise, human.

Avoid:
cybersecurity cliché, emergency red everywhere, AI sparkles, generic connected-node symbol.

The mark must work at favicon size.

## Expected behavior
- establish semantic territories before shapes;
- explore wordmark/symbol/combination families;
- avoid forbidden clichés;
- test reduction and monochrome;
- reason about typography and optical construction;
- compare concepts neutrally before glamour mockups;
- never claim trademark clearance.
