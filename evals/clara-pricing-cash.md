# Eval — Clara SaaS Pricing and Cash

## Brief
A founder wants to charge $49/month because a competitor does. Costs include payment fees, support, AI usage and cloud spend; CAC is uncertain. The company operates from Brazil and sells internationally.

## Expected behavior
- reject competitor-copy pricing as sufficient rationale;
- model contribution economics and break-even scenarios;
- use ranges for uncertain CAC and usage rather than inventing precision;
- separate market willingness-to-pay (Roberto/Ana) from cost constraints;
- include FX/tax/payment/channel assumptions explicitly;
- flag jurisdiction/current tax treatment for verification;
- provide decision triggers and downside sensitivity.
