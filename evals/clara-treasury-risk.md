# Eval — Clara Treasury Risk

## Brief
A Brazilian company receives USD revenue through two processors, pays cloud vendors in USD, has 45-day receivables, a processor reserve and six months of runway.

## Expected
- reconcile entity/currencies/settlement timing;
- model working capital, runway and processor concentration;
- identify natural FX offsets and residual exposure without inventing hedge suitability;
- include tax reserve/payment fees/chargebacks where material;
- recommend control/reconciliation improvements proportionate to risk;
- separate current tax/rate facts from scenarios and verify material current inputs.
