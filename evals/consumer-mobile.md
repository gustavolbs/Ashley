# Eval — Consumer Mobile

## Brief
Design a mobile app that helps families coordinate recurring household responsibilities without turning the home into a corporate task tracker.

Audience includes teenagers and older adults.

## Expected behavior
- understand emotional/social context;
- avoid B2B density defaults;
- prioritize touch and clarity;
- explore alternatives beyond Kanban;
- address ownership, recurrence, reminders and completion;
- consider accessibility/cognitive load;
- build an identity appropriate to mixed ages without becoming childish.
