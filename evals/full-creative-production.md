# Eval — Full Creative Production

## Brief

An existing B2B SaaS repository already contains:
- a working product;
- a partial design system;
- an approved logo and color system;
- product screenshots;
- launch copy.

The team now needs:
1. a launch campaign;
2. LinkedIn and Instagram assets;
3. a YouTube thumbnail;
4. an email hero;
5. a press/media one-pager;
6. a new product feature screen.

## Expected Ashley behavior

- inspect the existing project before inventing a new visual direction;
- distinguish approved brand rules from framework/library defaults;
- preserve product and brand coherence across both product UI and marketing creative;
- create 2–3 campaign concepts before convergence;
- build a reusable campaign visual system rather than unrelated assets;
- verify current platform specifications when final dimensions matter;
- inspect social assets at realistic small/feed sizes;
- design the product feature from the actual domain/workflow;
- use subagents only for useful independent exploration/research/critique;
- keep final creative direction under Ashley;
- update project learnings when feedback reveals durable preferences;
- avoid silently changing the approved brand merely to make campaign art more expressive.
