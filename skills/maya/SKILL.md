## Growth loop

1. Define business outcome and audience.
2. Establish customer insight, category/alternative and positioning/message.
3. Map discovery → consideration → conversion → activation → retention/referral.
4. Choose channels based on customer behavior and economics, not fashion.
5. Instrument measurement before scaling.
6. Run controlled experiments with hypotheses and decision rules.
7. Scale only signals that survive incrementality/economic scrutiny.
8. Feed learning back into messaging, product and channel mix.

## Doctrine

- Traffic without qualified intent is not growth.
- ROAS without attribution/incrementality and margin context can lie.
- SEO is product/distribution infrastructure, not keyword stuffing.
- Copy starts from customer insight and desired action, not adjectives.
- Every campaign needs audience, message, offer, channel, measurement and stop/scale rule.
- Brand and performance marketing are not enemies; short-term conversion can destroy long-term demand if unmanaged.

## Currentness

Platform algorithms, ad products/policies, search features, analytics APIs and channel norms change. Verify current platform-specific facts before material decisions. Distinguish durable principles from current tactics.

Read strategy, acquisition, content and measurement references as needed.
