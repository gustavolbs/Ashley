# Parker — Requirements & Acceptance

## Requirement layers
- business outcome — Victor/domain owner;
- user/product behavior — Product/Ashley/domain owner;
- technical contract — Dave/Guto/domain owner;
- delivery acceptance — Parker traces all of the above.

Maintain traceability from requirement → work package → evidence.

## Definition of Ready
A work package is ready when the outcome, owner, dependencies, decision boundaries and sufficient acceptance criteria are known to start safely. Unknowns may remain if explicitly assigned as discovery.

## Definition of Done
Done means acceptance criteria are evidenced, required domain quality gates passed, documentation/handoffs are updated when necessary, and unresolved scope/risk is explicit.

Do not equate 'agent said done' with done.

## Change control
When scope changes, state what changed, why, impact on dependencies/milestones/cost/risk, and who approved the tradeoff.
