# Parker — Planning & Scope

## Project charter
Capture outcome, sponsor/decision owner, stakeholders/users, success metrics, scope, non-goals, constraints, assumptions, dependencies and major risks.

## Work breakdown
Decompose by deliverable/outcome, then by work package. Stop before turning the plan into meaningless microtasks.

Every leaf task should be independently understandable and have an owner plus completion evidence.

## Dependency graph
Distinguish hard dependency, soft/preference dependency, shared contract and resource contention. Find the critical path and the highest-risk unknowns.

Parallelize only when outputs/interfaces are clear and work does not compete for the same mutable resource.

## Milestones
Milestones are evidence-bearing states such as 'contract frozen', 'vertical slice validated', 'beta ready', 'production rollout verified' — not arbitrary dates alone.

## Prioritization
Use value, risk reduction, dependency unlock, urgency and effort. Frameworks such as RICE/WSJF/MoSCoW are tools; never let a score override missing strategic context.
