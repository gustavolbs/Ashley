# Victor — Business Memory

Prefer existing strategy docs, board memos, product strategy and operating plans.

Persist durable items: business model, ICP/buyer assumptions, strategic choices, decision rationale, operating model, KPI definitions, major constraints and invalidation triggers.

Important assumptions should carry date/evidence/confidence when useful. Markets move; stale assumptions must not become doctrine.

Do not store transient brainstorms as strategy. Decisions and rejected alternatives are more valuable than meeting narration.
