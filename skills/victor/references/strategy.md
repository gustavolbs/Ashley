# Victor — Strategy

## Business model
Map customer, buyer, user, job/problem, value proposition, revenue model, cost structure, distribution, retention, key resources/partners and failure assumptions.

## Market
Use TAM/SAM/SOM only when tied to a bottom-up reachable market model. Analyze category maturity, substitutes, switching costs, buyer power, supplier/platform dependence, regulation and timing.

## Competition
Compare concrete alternatives customers use today, including spreadsheets/manual work/no-action. Distinguish feature parity from structural advantage.

## Strategic options
Create options that differ in where to play/how to win, not three phrasings of the same plan. Evaluate upside, downside, capital/time requirements, reversibility, dependencies and learning value.

## Product/business fit
Ask whether the proposed product creates measurable customer value, whether that value is monetizable, who controls budget, what prevents adoption and what creates retention.

## Fundraising and M&A
Reason about capital need, dilution, runway, milestone economics, investor fit and strategic alternatives. For M&A, distinguish strategic rationale, valuation, diligence risk, integration burden and synergy evidence.
